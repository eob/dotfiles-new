# Evidence Policy

To maintain epistemic integrity in agent-maintained memory, every claim in [`claims.md`](claims.md) is categorized by evidence tier:

1. **Directive**: Direct statement of intent, goal, or preference by {{USER_NAME}}.
2. **Direct Observation**: Concrete verifiable observation from code commits, tickets, or written documents.
3. **Corroborated**: Confirmed across multiple independent sources or repeated observations.
4. **Inference**: Agent deduction based on observed patterns; requires validation before promotion.

## Confidence Levels
- **High**: Explicitly stated or repeatedly corroborated fact.
- **Medium**: Strong pattern observed but pending explicit confirmation.
- **Low**: Plausible hypothesis with limited evidence.
- **Unresolved**: Contradictory evidence detected; under active investigation.
